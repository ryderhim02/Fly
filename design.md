# Skybound Expeditions

Fantasy: a social flying exploration game with the repeatable collect → bank → upgrade → reach farther loop of Bee Swarm Simulator and Pet Simulator 99, but built around movement and discovery.

## Loops
- **Seconds:** fly through a rift to harvest energy; the nearer the center, the faster the burst.
- **Minutes:** return to the Hangar, bank energy, and buy the next upgrade or unlock.
- **Hours:** unlock five biomes, complete milestone cards, defeat the weekly Sky Titan, and prestige into permanent mastery.

## Content commitment
Zones: Meadow Hangar (safe hub), Verdant Canopy (1,000 coins), Ember Crater (7,500), Moonlit Ruins (60,000), Astral Spire (500,000).
Upgrades: Wing Span I–V (harvest radius), Jetstream I–V (flight speed), Resonance I–V (energy multiplier), Cargo I–V (bank capacity), plus 3 prestige ranks.
Rifts: Meadow Spark, Canopy Bloom, Ember Core, Moon Relic, Astral Heart; each has a distinct value and unlock gate.
Endgame: mastery prestige resets spendable coins/upgrades while permanently increasing all future earnings; global Sky Titan score leaderboard hook remains available.

## Economy
Start with 0 coins and a 50-energy cargo hold. Rift energy values are 1, 4, 18, 90, 500. Banking converts 1 energy to 2 coins. Upgrade prices by tier are 50, 150, 450, 1,350, 4,050 for each branch; zone gates are 1,000 / 7,500 / 60,000 / 500,000. First upgrade is reachable in roughly 60–90 seconds.

## Multiplayer
All progression is per-player and persisted by user id. Rifts are shared landmarks so multiple flyers visibly gather together; each player’s energy, upgrades, mastery, and bank are independent. The hub is always a safe recovery path.

## First 60 seconds
Spawn at the Hangar with an arrow toward Meadow Spark. Fly through it until the hold fills, return to the Hangar, bank, and buy Wing Span I. The HUD always shows energy, coins, the next upgrade, and the next zone goal.
